Privacy Policy

Italian Recipes is a small informational website. We do not run user accounts and we do not ask for your name, address, or payment details.

The calculators and tools on this site run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored on our servers.

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. We do not sell or share personal information. Contact us at contact@italianrecipes.github.io with any privacy question.
